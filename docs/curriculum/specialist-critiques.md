## 1. Curriculum planner — independent critique

**Finding:** a prescribed chain of dozens of new themes risks replacing musical depth and pupil autonomy with coverage. Revisit a small number of relationships across listening, playing and making. The proposed four-family, four-spiral envelope is a planning choice, not an efficacy result. Calendar position must not become a level number or mandatory prerequisite.

**Concrete objection:** a rhythm practice score says little about phrase understanding or instrumental transfer; every spiral needs meaningful experiences across the eight strands. New context should mean a genuine musical change or relevant repertoire, not only a longer pentatonic pattern.

**Source correction:** the official framework page includes older 2016–2017 subject guidance. Do not call its music wheel/four instrumental phases new 2024 specifications. This affects how the earlier Theme 03 literature rationale is described.

**Recommendation:** keep Echo as a reusable introductory/return practice component, with creation accessible immediately. Treat anticipation and longer retention as optional specialist-reviewed extensions. Observe pupils explaining, predicting, revising and playing away from the screen before age-suitability claims.

**Author response:** adopted the four recurring families with a flexible 38-opportunity envelope and an eight-strand map. Explicitly separated spirals, activity levels and practice counts. Corrected source attribution in the evidence brief. Left annual dosage, actual educational efficacy and pupil suitability unvalidated.

## 2. Theory/composition — independent critique

**Finding:** all ten worked accepted sets match their declared constraints. Corrected example 7 is consistent: G4 lasts half a beat from offset 3.5, E4 starts at 4, D4 at 5 and C4 spans 6–8. B alone satisfies the named anticipatory entry; A/C shift the whole reply by half a beat. This is an event audit, not proof that every listener hears the distinction.

**Concrete objections:** example 4's “Use two two-note pieces” followed by P/Q/S can imply only two choices exist. Clarify “Choose two of these three pieces” in a future pupil brief. A contour, density or ending-note criterion is valid only under its exact wording: a named C ending does not establish universal closure; a two-attack reply with a rest is not the same rule as two half notes without rests. Open creation has multiple possibilities, not an aesthetic accepted set.

**Echo critique:** listening and construction share the same exact target. Creation permits real pitch/rest invention, so it is unfair to call it literally another echo test, but its fixed rhythm and lack of taught call/reply relationships limit the broader claim. Current level jumps alter several demands together; they are not a controlled developmental sequence.

**Recommendation:** preserve useful exact-comparison code and technical boundaries, re-scope its educational role later, and teach purposeful retained/changed relationships with curated examples before generator expansion. No feature or registry change was requested by this review.

**Author response:** the worked adjudication uses the clearer piece-choice instruction without changing PR #9. Retained a fair description of creation; explicitly analysed the level jumps. Rejected automatic scoring of expressive effect or a universal tonic preference. Echo re-scoping remains a future authorised task, with compatibility review.

## 3. Game and inclusive interaction — independent critique

**Finding:** three interfaces do not guarantee three learning operations. A matching choice and a unique-piece ordering may reward elimination of the same target. A free menu is also not equivalent to authentic informal learning. The cited Green source was an interview, not a full-book or application-efficacy study.

**Concrete objections:** level 4 can suggest creativity is earned even when technically unlocked; a long plan can create catch-up pressure; note colours/labels may reveal answers; a timed tap can measure latency/dexterity; a four-beat creative brief can be mistaken for an aesthetic verdict. A percussion adaptation cannot substantiate a melodic outcome. Local saved work must not be presented as a cloud portfolio.

**Recommendation:** distinguish hearing/predicting, constructing a relationship and ungraded making/revising; maintain all three visible entrances and creation at every enabled configuration. Use optional sounded demonstrations, repeatable audition, non-drag controls and no speed/streak pressure. Do not remove access aids to raise difficulty. Give meaningful short exits and instrument adaptations with explicit limits.

**Pilot proposal:** a deliberately varied small group can expose whether pupils restate the rule, locate replay/stop, change level, enter creation, revise and transfer. Serious access or data-loss problems cannot be averaged into satisfaction. The suggested sample and timing are design assumptions, not validated population thresholds.

**Author response:** adopted these interaction/access boundaries and concrete observation probes in the template and rubric. Pupil-selected context, help and partner exchange remain part of the framework, with solo/technical limits explicit. No app interaction changed here.

## 4. QA and evidence — independent critique

**Finding:** code checks, decoded signal, listening, human teacher review, learner/device observation and release evidence are distinct. PR #9's final-document parser checks multiple representations; its browser audio test samples pitches and locates the G attack within 75 ms. It does not independently establish all note durations, final sustain, perceptual clarity or pedagogical validity.

**Concrete objections:** a canonical JSON can be internally correct while final wording is incomplete; a hash alone can bind the wrong recording; high-volume generator checks can repeatedly enforce an ambiguous rule. Practice counters cannot certify conversation mastery. Review of source/tests is not a rerun, and model critiques cannot satisfy the human-teacher requirement.

**Recommendation:** require explicit pass/hold conditions and precise claims per evidence layer; check final prose/events/answers/recording separately. Preserve version-bound evidence and all FAIL/BLOCKED paths. Hold unresolved ambiguity, inaccessible core operation, data loss and unsupported release claims. No browser-policy bypass or skipped tests is an acceptable solution to a missing result.

**Author response:** adopted M1–M12 and separate design/implementation/release decisions. The worked case explicitly limits the existing audio check. Persistence and exact-output school-package requirements remain intact; old PR #9 test results are not represented as tests of this main-based documentation branch.

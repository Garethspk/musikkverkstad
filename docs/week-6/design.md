## Intended variety and bounded reuse

R3 intentionally reuses the actual Theme02 repair controls/sampled-piano engine,
with curated reproducible members of its real generator domain and a weekly pulse-
through-rests question. C2 intentionally reuses actual Formverkstad construction,
now focusing on base→changed-ending variation (A–A′/B–B′), with optional return
(ABA/BAB). These are scoped access to reviewed mechanics, NOT claims of new engines
or eighteen unique six-week mechanics. Their timing/map relationships remain exact.
E3 adds comparing expressive PURPOSES and articulation joins/accents over the SAME
phrase, then revising a plan. It differs from Week4's per-section dynamics/ramp
storyboard and Week5's four-part layer allocation. No aesthetic answer key.

Each is a separate optional3–5minute entrance, with direct recommended-case access,
current case number and stopping after one repair/construction/comparison. No timer,
tour, prior-week lock, compulsory three-slot session or automatic promotion. Four
programme configurations offer real named support; source activities retain their
own independent school-enabled levels. Do not equate programme config with source
level or silently enable a blocked source level. New E3 remains usable if sources off.

## E3 — interpretation workshop, six complete original contexts

Four note events `{notes,ticks}`, half-beat ticks total8. Pitch/rhythm remain IDENTICAL
between interpretations and never become editable. Plan fields EXACT
`{purpose,dynamic,joins,accents}`: purpose gentle|decisive|open (intended character,
not a correct emotion); dynamic p|mf|f; joins3 booleans (connected into next note vs
separated before next); accents4 booleans (local emphasis of that note's ATTACK).
Connected means planned minimal separation, not a tie/glissando or a single sustained
note across repeated pitches. Every written note remains a distinct attack. No exact
sounding length, decibel, instrumental feasibility or guaranteed emotion is inferred.

All3×3×2³×2⁴=1152 well-formed plans/context are open/ungraded (6912 total).
Changing purpose changes ONLY that label, not parameters, note events or assessment.
Compare actual dynamic/joins/accent differences, choose an intention, alter a join,
accent or dynamic, keep before/Undo/save/reopen. NO Check button, grade or done credit.
All plans may be saved whether they match a reader's taste/intended character or not.
Models are authors' illustrative INTENTIONS, not universal mappings; quiet can be
clear/decisive and connected can be strong. No fixed gentle=p or decisive=f rule.

Notation below: J=three joins (1connected/0separate), A=four accent booleans.

These are six contexts, not claimed as six independent mechanics. The repeated-note,
short-long and held-ending cases go beyond mere transposition. Rendering must keep
augmentation dots beside noteheads and slur-like join cues distinct from ties; full
plain text names pitch, duration, join and accent at each event. Musical parameters
and intended character are separate. Fixed visual note durations are not performed
articulation timing. New E3 requires programme only, no source Form/Groove/Conversation
exercise or new audio engine. Text/visual work does not verify hearing/performance.

Support1 full role/notation legend;2 prepared model0 before snapshot;3 actual parameter
difference table;4 case choice. All controls/reference/models remain available at all
allowed configurations. Concrete teacher choice: interpret1, compare equal dynamics
but connected vs separated and first-note emphasis; choose your purpose, change one
join, compare and stop. Optional teacher-guided voice/appropriate instrument transfer
is neither recorded nor assessed. Mode/task/case changes reset unfinished draft;
explicit saved versions and historical progress remain. No free-text pupil identities.

## First independent critiques

- **Music:** the three tasks have distinct musical purposes; freeze exact fixtures,
  not just counts. Beats continue through rests and sustained notes; & labels are
  interchangeable. All-silent bars are valid creations. Half rests should use aligned
  half-bar spans and sit on a staff line. A note of matching duration is not silence.
  Written events/velocity/source hashes cannot establish audible/perceived quality.
- **Game/inclusive:** place/revise a count, choose a symbol/value, and open creation
  are genuinely different actions. Shared components are appropriate. Keep direct
  entry, short stops, text alternatives, free enabled configurations, no compulsory
  audio, no partner/recording/latency requirement, stable focus and reversible edits.
- **QA:** validate old schema shapes before migration; no implicit consent to new
  content. Keep strict archive types and atomic all-sibling imports, missing-field
  preservation and corruption/quota protection. Independently enumerate finite
  alternatives and test real compiled/preview/extracted/teacher-kit boundaries.

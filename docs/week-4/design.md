## 2. Dynamics storyboard — eight different ungraded briefs

Every storyboard displays an original phrase/section sequence of exact local musical
material, not decorative symbol buttons. Reuse A/B/Av/Bv quarter-note material and
existing reviewed models X=D4 E4 G4 A4, Y=A4 G4 E4 D4 (4quarters); no compulsory audio.
A segment's start/end dynamic is p/mf/f; gradual changes can be crescendo or decrescendo
and must use increasing/decreasing endpoints consistently. Optional accent on its FIRST
attack is a local emphasis, not 'play the entire section loud'. Constant plans have
equal endpoints. Pupils edit each segment, keep before, compare and revise/save; any
structurally coherent plan is valid/ungraded. No matching emotion as universal truth.

## 3. Rhythm tile constructor — variable-duration making

One4/4bar =8 half-beat ticks. Tiles q=quarter-note attack(2ticks),r=quarter rest(2),
e=eighth-note attack(1),er=eighth rest(1),h=half-note attack/hold(4),hr=half rest(4).
Each note tile is ONE attack with its whole duration, not repeated binary grid notes.
Select insertion position, add/move/remove duration tiles, undo and keep before/current.
No drag-only controls. Reject overflow with explanation; incomplete drafts remain editable.
A separate ungraded free mode allows every supported8tickbar, including silence.

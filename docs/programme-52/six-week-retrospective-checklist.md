# First six weeks: retrospective and human validation checklist

**Documentation only; not a completed evaluation or a release adapter.** All human
validation below is **PENDING**. Pupil release **BLOCKED**. No teacher contact,
pupil trial, distribution, publication or deployment is authorised by this checklist.
Native Chromium and AI critiques cannot complete human rows. A future pupil trial
requires separate approval and its own appropriate arrangements.

## Implemented coverage to examine

These are eighteen independently accessible slots, **not eighteen unique mechanics**.
Theme02 and Formverkstad are intentionally reused; a weekly launch does not establish
transfer, learning, classroom usefulness or the quality of every source exercise.

| Week | Three slots and learning operations | Reuse / differentiation to examine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pulse detective: locate underlying beats; Symbol mechanic: choose duration symbol; Rhythm creator: construct | Separate counting, symbol selection and making |
| 2 | Form detective: compare sections; Melody contour: trace direction; Practice-plan challenge: choose/revise approach | Actual Form puzzle; visual contour and ungraded planning |
| 3 | Melodic response: retain/change ending; Notation debugger: repair notation; Form construction: contrast and return | Actual Conversation and Form engines; interactive repair |
| 4 | Return paths: investigate structure; Dynamics storyboard: compare section expression; Rhythm tiles: construct duration budget | Form reuse, per-section dynamics and variable-duration making |
| 5 | Motif transformation: change a component; Staff landmarks: place pitches; Ensemble roles: allocate parts | Pitch/rhythm edits, treble staff, layered entries/space |
| 6 | Groove repair: restore a pattern; Form arranger: build variation or return; Interpretation scenario: revise articulation/accents | Actual Theme02 repair, actual Form construction, fixed-phrase open expressive plan |

Review rhythm/pulse, melody, notation, form, expression and ensemble breadth. Count
musical operations and pupil choices, not screens, transpositions or case totals.
Compare Week6 against Weeks1–5: is changed-ending variation and fresh-attack phrasing
useful reinforcement, or does classroom evidence call for a future revision?
Do not implement such revisions merely by checking this document.

## Musical and content review — human PENDING

- [ ] PENDING: instructions, visual notes/rests, pitch names, MIDI/event sequences,
  rendered/sampled audio where present, exact correct-answer criteria and
  counterexamples agree for each objective brief. Record exact cases and candidate.
- [ ] PENDING: open tasks admit all structurally valid plans; no emotional/aesthetic
  preference is scored. Compare prepared models and pupil explanations critically.

  gate lengths are not described as exact performed note lengths.

  labelled relationships, not ties. Dots, durations, metre and onsets are correct.
- [ ] PENDING: source visual hints are supported practice, not proof of hearing;

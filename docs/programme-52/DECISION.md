# Issue #19 — Musikkverkstad 52-week programme decision

**Status: design proposal only, 23 October 2026.** No new activities implemented. Human teacher/educational/accessibility/device and pupil reviews remain PENDING. Pupil release BLOCKED. PR #15 and PR #9 are draft and untouched; Formverkstad is in main.

## Recommendation

Design **38 core opportunities and 14 optional exploration/revisit opportunities**, most with **three independent, mixed-modality activity slots** (156 slots total). Start with a working taxonomy of approximately **24 distinct activity types**, implemented with fewer reusable interaction mechanics. An activity slot is NOT a unique game or completed content. Avoid calendar locks, mandatory weekly homework, progress-based unlocking or teacher administration.

Each week's loose musical connection combines different objectives and interactions (e.g. visual form puzzle + text-based notation decision + creative composition), instead of one full week of listening followed by one of notation. Listening is neither compulsory nor the privileged learning outcome. The four curriculum families are behind-the-scenes coverage and spiral-planning lenses. A pupil can enter any school-enabled level.

## Decisions and boundaries

1. Teacher-recommended default is **one 3–5-minute exercise**, with a useful stopping point; optional longer free exploration.
2. Prioritise educationally meaningful variety, musical literacy, active making, rhythm, notation, form, instruments, expression and contextual understanding, as well as some listening.
3. Implement reusable controls (classify, compare, sequence, rearrange, spot/fix, choose under a rule, compose, snapshot/revise), sharing accessible components rather than building 24 separate apps.
4. Maintain clear validated rules for objective tasks, multiple acceptable solutions where applicable, and no score for aesthetics or open creation.
5. All new content needs musical event/visual/text consistency, exact valid sets, semantic NN/NB/EN, keyboard/touch and text alternatives, tested persistence and migration, and precise teacher relevance. Avoid unlicensed photos/recordings and superficial cultural stereotypes.
6. Develop a **six-week vertical programme slice** (18 slots) before scaling. This is a production backlog, not a demand for immediate implementation of all 18 slots.
7. Review educational, music/composition, game/inclusive and QA perspectives separately; model-based reviews cannot confer human approval. Gather real teacher acceptance TA1–TA5 at a coherent multi-activity milestone.

## Current integration status

On main: Themes 01–02 and auxiliary visual Formverkstad. The combined Theme 03 conversations implementation remains on open draft PR #15, not main. An older Echo-only draft PR #9 remains unmerged. Planning documents must not describe either draft as released content.

## Critical trade-off

A fixed 52×3 grid is an editorial planning instrument, not evidence that 156 engaging exercises exist. Keep all unbuilt slots PROPOSED. A well-tested and recognisably musical 6-week experience is more valuable than automatically producing 52 repetitive shells. Suggested initial distinctness target: twelve or more genuinely different task/source combinations per reusable mechanic/configuration where applicable, reviewed before further scaling; **this number is an engineering hypothesis, not an empirically validated dosage**.
